Four Stages, One Seamless Hand-off
1
Lender submits, as usual

A lender uploads documents in JetDocs, exactly like today. The files land in a temporary folder, and our orchestration service notices the new submission right away — nobody has to go looking for it.
2
A secure hand-off into Azure

The documents travel over a private, dedicated connection into Azure — never the open internet — and the service proves who it is before anything is allowed to move.
3
Azure reads and makes sense of it

Each document is sorted by type (Note, Deed of Trust, Title Commitment, Survey, Closing Instructions), read for its text and layout, and the handful of details needed to raise an invoice are pulled out.
4
Invoice created, files delivered home

With that information in hand, the system creates the transaction in LoanTrack, gets the invoice number, and uploads the original documents into EDM under that number. From here, the existing process carries on unchanged.

Why It's Worth Doing
Three small shifts that add up to a much smoother start for every submission.
1
No more manual re-keying
Coordinators no longer open every document just to create an invoice number — the metadata is captured automatically.
2
Faster, more consistent hand-offs
Classification and extraction happen in seconds and the same way every time, instead of depending on who's at the desk.
3
Nothing downstream has to change
ClosingExpress, SecureDocs, and the MS Access queue keep working exactly as they do today — this simply feeds them faster.
